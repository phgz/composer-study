## Techniques

1. **A slow bass with a late step inside the bar.** The low track repeatedly holds its first pitch through two quarter-note positions and changes on the third, while the upper part subdivides the space.
   *Works on:* rhythm, texture.
   *Why:* the upper line's changing subdivisions do not determine where the low line moves; the two remain separately traceable. **[score: P.198, opening]** *(from the score)*
   *How:* give the bass a long-short tread, then compose a tune whose arrivals sometimes agree with its late step and sometimes precede it.
   *Where:* P.198, labelled bars 1–4 and 6 in the MIDI reading. **[score: P.198]**
2. **An aria that already contains ornament and contrast.** The first upper bar moves in quarters; the next inserts short turning notes; later the upper line holds while an inner line enters beneath it.
   *Works on:* melody, texture.
   *How:* make the plain statement a shaped tune with its own quick detail and held arrivals, rather than an uninflected skeleton waiting to become music in its variations.
   *Where:* P.198, labelled bars 1–3 and 9–10. **[score: P.198]**
3. **The supporting line is a melody too.** Near the end of the opening, the bass climbs through a repeated note and descends by steps before reaching the cadence.
   *Works on:* melody, harmony.
   *How:* preserve a few arrival points in a returning bass, but connect them with direction; do not equate a variation form with an identical looping bass.
   *Where:* P.198, labelled bars 9–12. **[score: P.198]**

## Numbers

The reading contains 1,329 note events across five MIDI tracks. These are not asserted to be five independent voices. The measurements and source folder are in [the dated reading record](../checks/piece76-reading-2026-11-26.json).

## What he does not do

In this opening, the bass is neither a drone nor an unbroken run of equal short notes.
