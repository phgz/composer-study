# Steve Reich (born 1936)

Begun for piece 77 on 25 November 2026. The scope is rhythmic construction and the handover between voices and percussion in *Drumming* and *Music for Pieces of Wood*. No measured profile or readable score for this unit was present in the project.

Project use and reception are recorded in [composer-blend](https://github.com/phgz/composer-blend/blob/claude/work/reference/composers/steve-reich.md).

## Techniques

1. **Build and unbuild a rhythm.** Keep a cycle running and bring one of its silent positions to life on successive passes. The process can run backwards.
   *Works on:* rhythm, texture.
   *How:* choose the complete figure and the places it will occupy. Add a single attack per pass and later remove attacks from the same figure.
   *Where:* the opening and reductions in *Drumming*. *Music for Pieces of Wood* also uses gradual additions. [Reich, programme notes]

2. **Give the next sound the same pattern before the first sound leaves.** The new players enter quietly on the notes and rhythm that are already present.
   *Works on:* colour, texture, form.
   *Why:* a section can change its instrumental sound continuously while the pitch and rhythm carry on through the join. [Reich, *Drumming*] *(his words)*
   *How:* let both groups share the figure momentarily. Change their relative prominence and then withdraw the first group.
   *Where:* the transitions from drums to marimbas and from high marimbas to low glockenspiels in *Drumming*. [Reich, programme notes]

3. **Let a voice take on an instrument's sound.** He describes the singers imitating the instruments in the ensemble.
   *Works on:* colour, texture.
   *How:* give a singer an instrumental figure heard just beforehand, with an articulation that lets the two inhabit the same line.
   *Where:* *Drumming*, particularly the women's voices with marimbas. [Reich, programme notes]

## Walks through whole works

In *Drumming* the drum ensemble gives way to marimbas and voices. The centre of the sound then moves to glockenspiels with piccolo and whistling. All the groups come together in the last part. This is the sequence in the composer's programme note.

## Numbers

Reich describes three successively shorter metres in *Music for Pieces of Wood*, 6/4, 4/4 and 3/4, for five pairs of tuned claves. [Reich, 2010 programme]

## What he does not do

In the described *Drumming* joins, the new instrument starts by sharing the preceding group's pattern and register.
