# The bracket test (24 November 2026)

**Why:** Philip, on using the outside ears as a stand-in for the listener: "Somehow, I feel like music is all about predictability and its opposite. Maybe we should aim to have a score in a certain optimal bracket."

**What the research says:**
- [Gold et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6867811):
  - Liking of real melodies (about 30 seconds each, monophonic, many genres) followed an inverted U. It was highest at intermediate surprise, with surprise and uncertainty computed by IDyOM, a statistical model of the same kind as composer-study's ear.
  - A marginal interaction in their first study: the inverted U held where uncertainty was low.
- [Cheung et al. 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31708393/):
  - About 80,000 chords from 745 Billboard songs. Pleasure was highest for a surprising chord where the listener was sure what was coming, or a likely one where they were unsure.
  - So the finding is surprise against uncertainty, not one bracket.

**Why our earlier null result does not settle it:** piece 44's M0 and `study/rated.py` tested straight lines. An inverted U gives a straight-line correlation near zero. So "surprise does not predict Philip's ratings" did not rule a bracket out, and Claude had described it too strongly.

**Two cautions, from Claude:**
- **A piece's average hides its course.** Philip's own words are "on the edge of being obvious at every second", which is about the course of a piece; test 3 below looks at that.
- **A band is not a recipe.** Composing toward a number would make a new kind of generic. If a band is found, it is for finding where a piece leaves it.

## Design (`bracket-2026-11-24/prepare_bracket.py`, `compare_bracket.py`)

- **The pieces:** every piece Philip has rated with a single mark, 42 in all.
  - The 30 of `data/rated.json`.
  - Pieces 63 to 71 (12, from composer-blend's CATALOGUE.md on 24 November). Their notes come from each notes module's EV rows; `study/pieces.py` now reads those.
- **The notes:**
  - Every pitched part. A drum's part is left out by its name.
  - A pitch struck twice at once by two parts is heard once.
  - All on the piano, as in every AMT test so far: AMT hears notes and rhythms, not the orchestration.
- **The ear:** AMT, every note, no given notes. For each note: its pitch's bits (surprise, S), and the entropy of AMT's guess at the pitch (uncertainty, H). Keeping the entropy is new in `study/amt_score.py`, as a third number for each note.
- **The tests:** three, each against 10,000 shuffles of the ratings, one-sided. A test passes under 0.05 / 3. AMT Large decides; AMT Medium is reported beside it.
  1. **The inverted U (after Gold):** the rating fitted to each piece's mean S and its square. Passes if the square's coefficient is below 0. Its peak is reported, and whether it falls among the pieces.
  2. **Surprise against uncertainty (after Cheung):** a piece's share of notes that are either sure and surprised (H under the median of every note, S over) or unsure and unsurprised. Passes if a higher share goes with a higher rating (Spearman).
  3. **The bracket at every second:** each second's mean S over the notes begun within two seconds of it. The band runs from the 25th to the 75th percentile of every second of every piece. Passes if a higher share of a piece's seconds in the band goes with a higher rating.
- **For the record:** the straight-line correlations of mean S and mean H with the ratings.

## Predictions, written before anything was scored (Claude's, not Philip's)

1. **None of the three tests passes.** The reasons:
   - 42 pieces of every kind, each rated after one or two hearings.
   - An ear that hears every orchestra as a piano.
   - A range of ratings mostly from 3 to 5.
   - A test that fails here does not show the bracket is false. A test that passes would only be worth checking on the pieces still to come, scored before Philip rates them, not a rule.
2. **Test 1's coefficient below 0,** the inverted U's direction, but not passing.
3. **The straight-line correlations near 0** (|rho| under 0.3), as before.

## Results (scored on Philip's Mac, 24 November)

**Health:** AMT Large scored 0.56 to 2.80 bits a note token (median 1.51); entropy 0 to 5.97 bits a note (mean 1.21). That is 44 inputs, 82,436 notes.

| | AMT Large | AMT Medium |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Inverted U: the square's coefficient (p) | +0.0012 (0.49) | +0.0007 (0.49) |
| 2. Surprise against uncertainty: rho (p) | 0.09 (0.28) | 0.19 (0.11) |
| 3. The bracket at every second: rho (p) | -0.10 (0.73) | -0.09 (0.71) |
| Straight lines: mean S, mean H | 0.13, 0.08 | 0.10, 0.07 |

**The predictions:**
1. **None passes:** held.
2. **Test 1's coefficient below 0:** failed. It is flat (+0.001): no curve either way.
3. **The straight lines near 0:** held.

**What the run shows about the design:**
- **A piece's mean surprise and its mean uncertainty are nearly one number.** For example, 0.39 and 0.38 for piece 52b, 2.05 and 1.82 for 64b. On average, a model that is right about its own doubt is as surprised as it is unsure. At the scale of a whole piece, H adds nothing to S.
- **Test 2 was close to empty.** With every note's medians at S 0.21 and H 0.80, only 1% to 8% of a piece's notes fall where Cheung found pleasure. AMT is almost never surprised where it was sure. A better measure would be each note's surprise beyond what its uncertainty predicts. It was not fixed beforehand, so it was not run.
- **What AMT's surprise mostly measures across these pieces is repetition.**
  - Least surprising: 52b (0.39, rated 4.5) and 30 (0.42, rated 3.5), both built on ostinati and returns.
  - Most surprising: 56 (2.56, rated 4) and 64b (2.05, rated 4.5).
  - The pieces rated 5 spread from 1.19 (64) to 1.90 (29). Piece 60, rated 1, sits in the middle (1.34).

**The decision:** the bracket, measured this way (AMT, whole pieces, every orchestra on a piano), does not show in Philip's ratings. That does not rule it out: a listener's expectation is not AMT's, and the bracket may hold moment by moment, against what the listener expected just then. It goes no further unless Philip wants the better test 2 run.
